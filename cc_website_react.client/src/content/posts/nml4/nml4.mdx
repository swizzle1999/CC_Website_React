The Highlands Major is back!

No Man's Land IV is coming 12th September 2026 - featuring SIX huge titles. 

Street Fighter 6
Tekken 8
Granblue VS Rising 
Guilty Gear Strive
Super Smash Bros Melee (Singles & Doubles) 
And last but not least, Divekick. 

We are super excited to be bringing forward a lot of your favourite things from last year, including the Bounty System and improved space for TCG/Boardgames. Alongside this we are looking at additional side events, keep your eyes peeled for details on this! 

This year we are really pushing to make No Man's Land an unmissable experience - whether you are a top player or just a fan of fighting games, we will have something for you!
